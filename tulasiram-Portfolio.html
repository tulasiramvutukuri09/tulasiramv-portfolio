Adaptive Smart Odor Neutralization System with Environmental Calibration

Application No. 202641059084
Journal No. 24/2026
Filed 08 May 2026
Published 12 Jun 2026

An IoT-enabled system that detects and neutralizes harmful gases and odors in indoor and industrial spaces. It pairs gas and environmental sensors with an embedded controller that recalibrates itself against temperature and humidity drift, then drives a hybrid neutralization setup — extraction, ventilation, and filtration — in a closed loop, adjusting airflow and duration to how severe the pollution actually is, with real-time alerts and cloud dashboard monitoring throughout.

Batch No. MI2140 · Minor Project-II

Smart IoT-Based Odor Neutralizer System

Summary

A real-time, automated air-quality system built to replace manual monitoring, chemical sprays, and basic ventilation. An MQ-135 gas sensor and DHT11 sensor feed continuous readings to an ESP32, which compares them against safe thresholds. When odor levels cross the limit, the ESP32 drives a DC motor through a MOSFET circuit to suction out polluted air while a buzzer alerts anyone nearby — then switches itself off once air quality recovers. A WiFi-hosted dashboard exposes live sensor data and pump status for remote monitoring.
